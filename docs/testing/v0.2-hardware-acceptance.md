# v0.2.0 physical hardware acceptance test (reference Raspberry Pi)

Status: **In progress.** The v0.1 stability test was **ended early on purpose, with maintainer
approval** (18 Nov 2026), and the maintainer installed v0.2.0 on the Pi. The first readings are in
[v0.2 physical results](v0.2-physical-results.md). They are initial results, not final
acceptance. Any further change to the running installation (restart, option change, update)
needs the maintainer's approval.

Covers roadmap criteria OB-2, OB-9, OB-10 and OB-13. Record results with no household identifiers:
use `<lan-ip>` and never paste tokens, fingerprints or identity hashes. Each step lists its pass
condition. A step without evidence is "not run", never "passed".
